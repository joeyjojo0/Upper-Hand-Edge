What each factor measures
Trend
Price vs its 20-day average in ATRs, and whether the 20-day sits above the 50-day.
Momentum
Daily RSI(14), centred on 50.
London drive
Today's London move, scaled by the average daily range, multiplied by how often NY has followed (or faded) London in this market.
Structure
Where price sits inside yesterday's range. Above the high scores +1, below the low −1.
Macro
Dollar index for USD pairs and metals, VIX for indices and crypto.
Positioning
CFTC speculator positioning at 3-year extremes, read as a contrarian tilt.
Off-exchange
FINRA short-share z-score in the matching ETF (SPY, QQQ, DIA, IWM, GLD).

The score ranks setups. It does not predict price and it is not investment advice.
